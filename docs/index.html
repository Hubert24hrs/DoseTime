Privacy Policy for DoseAlert

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information DoseAlert ("we", "us") collects when you use the DoseAlert mobile app (the "App"), how it is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Your medications, schedules, dose history and caregiver contacts are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address, your name (if you provide it) and your sign-in method with Google Firebase Authentication.
  • We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app usage and fix crashes.
  • Pro purchases are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card or bank details.
  • The App does not show ads and does not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The App stores medication names, dosages, schedules, dose logs, reminder settings and contacts you add in a local database on your device. This data stays on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. You can also export or delete it from Manage → Data Management.

Account information (optional)

If you choose to create an account or sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (Google) to manage it. Depending on how you sign in, this includes:

  • Your email address and, if provided, your display name.
  • Your sign-in method (email and password, Google, or Apple) and a unique user ID.
  • If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", an Apple private relay address instead of your real email.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by the App. Medication data is not linked to or uploaded with your account.

Purchases

DoseAlert Pro is sold through Google Play billing. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID so the App can confirm your Pro status and restore purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the App is used and to fix problems. They may collect:

  • App interactions (for example, screens viewed and features used).
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and basic device information (model, OS version).
  • App instance and device identifiers generated by Firebase.

This information is not used for advertising. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App asks for notification, exact alarm and full-screen alarm permissions so it can ring when a dose is due, including on the lock screen. These permissions are used only for medication reminders.

How we use information

  • To provide reminders and the App's features.
  • To create and secure your optional account.
  • To verify Pro purchases.
  • To fix crashes and improve the App.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers named above, who process it on our behalf.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: kept until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Your account: kept until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time in Manage → Account → Delete account. You can also request deletion without the App. Deleting your account removes your Firebase account record. It does not delete medication data stored on your device.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept according to Google Firebase retention settings, at most 14 months for Analytics.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing and legal purposes.

Security

Data sent to Firebase and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Sensitive values on your device are kept in encrypted storage.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Medical disclaimer

DoseAlert is a reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
